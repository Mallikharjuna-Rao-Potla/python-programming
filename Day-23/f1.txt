Abhishek Sharma (born 4 September 2000) is an Indian cricketer who plays for the Indian national team. 
He is a batting all-rounder, who bats left-handed and bowls slow left-arm orthodox spin. 
He represents Punjab in domestic cricket and Sunrisers Hyderabad in the Indian Premier League.
Sharma was a member of the Indian squad that won the 2026 T20 World Cup and the 2025 Asia Cup.
On 17 September, 2026, during the 3rd T20I against Afghanistan, Sharma hit a century off 30 balls, his third in T20I's, and broke the record for the fastest T20I century by any player from a full member nation. He scored 108 runs of 34 balls, hitting 9 fours and 11 sixes[44] and celebrated the century with a yoga pose.
